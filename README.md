# CITADEL

**Defend your capsules. Curve your shots. Be the last one standing.**

CITADEL is a neon arcade survival game where one good return can turn the whole
match around. Slide your curved bat to protect your capsules, bend shots past
your rivals and bank the ball off the arena walls.

Lose a capsule and you get the next serve. Lose them all and you're out. As
fortresses fall, the board reshapes around the survivors and the balls keep coming.

- **100 levels of rising pressure.** Face tougher bots, changing arenas and up to
  sixteen balls at once.
- **Two to sixteen fortresses.** It's you against the bots, from a tense duel to
  a crowded arena with threats on every side.
- **Put some spin on it.** Flick into a return or serve to bend the flight. Find
  an opening, take a bank shot or turn a desperate save into an attack.
- **Keep your fortress alive.** Collect supply capsules to repair your defences
  while the rally is still in play.

## How to play

Move left and right to steer your bat. **Click, tap or press Space** to serve;
flick as you launch or return the ball to add curve. On a touchscreen, **drag to
move and tap to launch**. **A/D** or the **arrow keys** also steer.

Protect your capsules and break everyone else's. The last fortress standing wins.

Press **Escape/P** or the touch **Pause** button for a breather. Music and sound
effects have separate volume controls, and your progress saves in your browser.
